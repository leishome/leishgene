Leishgene indexes the genomes of the leishref catalog that record a GFF annotation, plus the annotations listed in data/extra/gff_overrides.yaml of the repository (currently Ld1S). Genomes of the catalog without an annotation are not searchable.

    What a result is

    • One row per gene. Transcripts, CDS and other child features are folded into their gene, and their product, EC, GO and Dbxref values are kept on it. Exons, UTRs, introns and similar rows are ignored.
    • Features without a gene parent (a lone tRNA or CDS) are rows of their own.
    • Assembly gaps are not indexed. Some EMBL-derived GFFs (GCA_916722125.1, GCA_900635355.2) write one gene row per feature of a locus (the locus, its transcript, :E1, :CDS1, :5'UTR…); rows with the same name that overlap or touch are merged into one gene.
    • Rows that repeat an ID are one record: a discontinuous pseudogene spans all its pieces, and the gene and rRNA rows of one Ld1S rRNA are merged.
    • Coordinates are 1-based and as in the GFF; the chromosome is the sequence name of that GFF. The length is end - start + 1 in bp, gaps included.

    What can be searched

    • Bare words match id, name, product, EC, GO, Dbxref, the remaining GFF attributes, and the genome, strain, alias and species names.
    • id name product type species genome ec go dbxref restrict a term to one field; genome: also matches the strain and the aliases.
    • The names index answers id/name/product searches at once; other fields load the genome files first.

    Known limits

    • Most catalog GFFs carry no EC numbers or GO terms, so ec: and go: only find genes of annotations that have them (Ld1S has GO terms, not EC numbers).
    • UniProt links come from the UniProtKB cross-references of the GFF, and UniParc links are searches on the protein accession. Genes without such references get no link.
    • No orthology or cross-genome grouping yet, and no sequences.
    • Ld1S: the assembly GCA_002243465.1 has no annotation at NCBI. The annotation shown here is the Ld1S.gff kept in the repository, whose sequences (1–36, maxi) were renamed to the NCBI names (CP022616.1–CP022652.1) of the same sequence. Its gene ids and descriptions are those of that file.
